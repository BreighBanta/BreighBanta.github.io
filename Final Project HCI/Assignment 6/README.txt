For this lab redesign, I wanted to add sound to make 
the color wheel feel more interactive. I felt that the 
design was pretty good starting out, but there were a 
few small things that I wanted to change.

I made sure to put more space between the wheel, the 
spin button, and the message box. I also wanted to make 
the button and message a little more visible by making
them bigger. 

For the sounds, I downloaded a sound file from a website
and used the syntax from previous projects to make a 
spinning sound play while the wheel was spinning.
