For this project, I wanted to combine both of my
majors. To achieve this, I changed parts of the 
design to look more like the barn at midway. I also
used different colored horses as the "characters."

At first, I wanted to use pictures of the horses
that are on Midways campus, and make each a different 
character. Due to time and other factors, I did not 
achieve this. I did however, use a picture of one
of midways barns as a background for the game.

As I began to change the games original design from
class, I started to make it feel more like an educational
game. I wanted to make sure to use distinct coat
colors and pictures to avoid confusion for the player. 
I also used existing speak function to say the name 
of each coat out loud for the player. I also wanted to 
display the name of the coat at the top of the screen.

Most of my changes were made to the background 
and card characters, so I made most of the changes
on my own. However when it came to displaying the name
and getting everything to speak correctly, I ran into 
some trouble. Because of the mix of picture extensions
I chose to make the extension part of the "character"
to allow the cards to correctly display. this caused issues
 that I had no idea to solve, so I did use
chatgpt to help walk me through the errors and 
explain why I was getting them in the first place.
